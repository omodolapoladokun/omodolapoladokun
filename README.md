## Selected Projects

### North America Retail Sales Analysis

**SQL | Data Modelling | Retail Analytics | Operations Analysis**

Analysed retail sales and operational data to investigate sales performance, customer segments, product performance and delivery efficiency.

**Selected findings**

* Average delivery time across the dataset was approximately **36 days**.
* Some products recorded delivery times of up to **214 days**.
* Chairs generated approximately **$36,471 in profit**.
* Corporate customers averaged approximately **35 days** in delivery time.

[View Project](https://github.com/royalpriestcode/North-America-Retail-Sales-Analysis)

### Amazon Marketplace Sales Analysis

**Excel | Commercial Analytics | Customer & Product Analysis**

Analysed **1,465 products** to examine the relationship between product characteristics, customer reviews, ratings, pricing and revenue potential.

**Selected findings**

* Electronics represented the strongest revenue opportunity, with more than **₹87M in potential revenue**.
* Review volume showed a stronger relationship with revenue than star ratings in the analysis.
