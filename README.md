## Selected Projects

### Digital Development in Africa

**Power BI | DAX | Development Analytics | Digital Infrastructure**

Analysed World Bank development data across **11 African countries from 2010–2024** to examine internet adoption, mobile connectivity, broadband access, electricity and secure internet infrastructure.

**Selected findings**

- Internet usage increased across the selected countries, but the pace of adoption remained uneven.
- Mobile connectivity was generally higher than actual internet usage, showing that mobile access does not always translate into full digital participation.
- Fixed broadband remained much lower than mobile connectivity across the selected countries.
- Countries with stronger electricity access generally appeared better positioned for stronger internet adoption.

[View Project](https://github.com/omodolapoladokun/Digital-Development-in-Africa)

### Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

**Excel | Customer Analytics | Retention Analysis | Business Intelligence**

Analysed **7,043 telecom customers** to identify the factors associated with customer churn and determine which customer groups were most at risk of leaving.

**Selected findings**

- **1,869 customers churned**, representing an overall churn rate of **26.5%**.
- Month-to-month customers recorded the highest churn.
- Customers with higher monthly charges and those using Electronic Check showed stronger churn patterns.
- Newer customers and customers without Technical Support were among the most vulnerable groups.

[View Project](https://github.com/omodolapoladokun/Telecom-Customer-Churn-Analysis)

### Apple Stock Time-Series Analysis

**Python | Pandas | Time-Series Analysis | Financial Analytics**

Analysed historical Apple stock data to examine price behaviour, returns, volatility, moving averages and trading activity across different time periods.

**Selected findings**

- Long-term price growth contained periods of negative returns and increased volatility.
- Daily returns provided a clearer view of short-term performance than closing prices alone.
- 7-day and 30-day moving averages helped separate short-term price movement from broader trends.
- Rolling volatility showed that the level of risk changed over time.

[View Project](https://github.com/omodolapoladokun/Apple-Stock-Time-Series-Analysis)

### Amazon Marketplace Sales Analysis

**Excel | Commercial Analytics | Customer & Product Analysis**

Analysed **1,465 products** to examine the relationship between product characteristics, customer reviews, ratings, pricing and revenue potential.

**Selected findings**

- Electronics represented the strongest revenue opportunity, with more than **₹87M in potential revenue**.
- Review volume showed a stronger relationship with revenue than star ratings in the analysis.
- Heavy discounts did not consistently translate into stronger commercial performance.
- Product categories varied substantially in pricing, review activity and estimated revenue potential.
